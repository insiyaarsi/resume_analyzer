Retail Store Supervisor — Customer Experience

Location: Mississauga
Type: Full-Time

About the Position
As a Retail Store Supervisor, you will oversee daily store operations, support sales associates, and ensure a positive customer experience. This is a hands-on leadership role.

Responsibilities

⦁	Supervise daily store activities, merchandising, and inventory

⦁	Train and guide sales associates in customer service best practices

⦁	Resolve escalated customer issues

⦁	Prepare sales reports and assist with scheduling

⦁	Ensure store visual standards are maintained

Required Skills

⦁	Strong interpersonal and leadership abilities

⦁	Customer-service oriented

⦁	Ability to multitask and handle fast-paced environments

⦁	Basic knowledge of POS systems and inventory software

Preferred

⦁	Previous supervisory experience in retail or hospitality
